About Me

Story behind the lens

I'm Alex Rivera — a visual creator with eight years of experience working at the intersection of photography, film, and brand storytelling. My work has appeared in Vogue, Wired, and across global brand campaigns.

I believe the best creative work comes from genuine collaboration. Every project starts with listening — to the client, the subject, and the environment — before a single frame is captured.
